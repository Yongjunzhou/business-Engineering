## 三、专业侧核账（三笔，全部合）

1. **"适宜性／存续性／合意性 vs 兼容与可行性"**——正文 14.2.3 引 *Architecture focuses on suitability, viability, and desirability, whereas design focuses on compatibility with technologies and other design elements and on feasibility of construction and integration.*，与 `15288:2023` §5.9（md 内 L1146 起该节，句在 L1156）**逐字一致**。
2. **"必要且充分的输入"**——14.2.4 引 *Confirmation is made that they are a necessary and sufficient response…and a necessary and sufficient input to other processes, in particular architecture and design.*，与 §6.4.3.3 **NOTE 18** 逐字一致，注号标对。
3. **"转换"与"实现级"**——§6.4.5.1（*This process transforms architecture and requirements into a design…*）与 §6.4.5.1 **NOTE 1**（*Design provides the 'implement-to' level…*）均逐字一致。
